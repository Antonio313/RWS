# RWS home page (single-page marketing site)

Scope: whole one-page site (src/App.tsx and sections). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: owners of small and growing businesses, worldwide, mostly non-technical. Job: recognise their problem, see the real build that fixed one like it, start a conversation.
Proof: real client builds and products (Pongs 80% figure); labelled demo fragments where screens don't exist. No prices, testimonials, invented stats, or country framing ("built in Jamaica", country lists).
Constraints: React 19 + Tailwind 4 + Vite; EmailJS form stays; first person, plain, no em dashes; outcome-led headline; not techy, not cheap or template-like. Keep the reveal and drift motion the owner liked.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site works like a friendly app a busy owner already trusts: you tap the problem slowing you down and see how it was fixed. Refuses both the dark developer studio and the generic split hero with a laptop mockup.
OWN-WORLD: Warm light stone ground (#F3F1EC), white cards with soft large corners and no borders or resting shadows, one saturated blue (#1F5EEB) only on things you can do, near-black warm ink, one friendly humanist sans (Figtree). Rounded "RWS" mark in the blue beside the full name. Real screens sit on tinted panels inside cards.
STORY: Visitor reads "Less admin. More business.", taps their problem, a sheet shows the real fix and who it was for, they tap "Fix mine" and land in a pre-filled enquiry; below, recent work, website vs system, the person who answers, budget, contact.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark nav with a blue pill "Start a project"; headline "Less admin. More business." large; one sentence; "What's slowing your business down?" over a 4-column grid of large white problem cards (8 shown, "Show 6 more" reveals the rest); tapping opens the fix sheet (right panel on desktop, bottom sheet on phone).
FORM: dealt challenger digital-design-canon-warm-consumer-app-surface (catalog), chosen by the owner over the assigned Swiss Timetable. seed:b971070b008a5e3a
Signature interaction: problem card opens the fix sheet; "Fix mine" closes it, pre-fills the form and scrolls to it.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
